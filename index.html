No.04 · Buyer Journey

Eight steps. Zero surprises.

Buying a home in this market without a defined process is how mistakes happen. Here's the path Vali walks every client through, in plain order.

01

Discovery Call

Thirty minute conversation about what you're solving for, when, and why. No pressure. We confirm fit before paperwork.

02

Pre-Approval

Connect with our trusted mortgage advisors. Know your real number before you fall in love with a property.

03

Search Setup

Custom MLS feed with your criteria. New listings hit your inbox within minutes of going live, not days later.

04

Showings & Tours

Vali tours with you. Notes on every property. Honest feedback on what works and what doesn't.

05

Offer Strategy

Comparable sales analysis, market read, condition recommendations. Your offer is built to win, not just submitted.

06

Negotiation

This is where eleven years of pattern recognition pays for itself. Vali negotiates the deal you didn't know was possible.

07

Inspection & Conditions

Vetted home inspector, lawyer, insurer. We project-manage the due diligence so you can focus on financing.

08

Closing & Beyond

Keys delivered. Moving checklist sent. Vali stays in your corner long after the transaction closes.
